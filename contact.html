Before You Write

Quick Answers

A few things that help us respond faster and more accurately.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

At minimum: the plot or L.R. number, the sub-county or nearest town, and a short description of what you want to do. If you have a title deed, deed plan, previous approvals or correspondence, mention it — we will ask you to share it at the next stage.

Do you give indicative costs before a site visit?

Yes — for most standard scopes, we can provide a cost band based on your location and project type without a site visit. Final figures are confirmed after field assessment.

Can I send documents before we have agreed on scope?

Yes. Send them to the official email address, and mention in the enquiry that you have already sent documentation. This shortens the scoping stage considerably.

Do you handle urgent or time-sensitive projects?

Where possible, yes. Flag the urgency clearly in your enquiry and we will tell you honestly whether the timeline you need is achievable given regulatory queues.
