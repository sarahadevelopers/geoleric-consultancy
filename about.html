Our Belief

We don't just map land. We help make sense of it.

Every project begins with understanding the physical environment, the available spatial information and the regulatory context. Our approach connects these elements to create clearer pathways from land information to development decisions.

The GeoLeric Method

From site to strategy — in five deliberate stages.

This is not just a list of services. It is the working sequence we move through on every project — so that technical work connects clearly to the decisions it enables.

Working Principle

Each stage produces a document, dataset or decision that the next stage depends on. Nothing is orphaned.

01 Understand

We begin with the site — its physical characteristics, your objectives, and any constraints that shape what is possible.

02 Analyse

Spatial information, land-use considerations and environmental context are compiled into a single working picture of the site.

03 Plan

We translate the analysed data into practical planning and layout solutions — clear enough to build against, robust enough to submit.

04 Navigate

We work through the relevant county and NEMA processes on your behalf — preparing submissions, responding to queries and following through.

05 Deliver

You receive clear technical outputs — drawings, reports, licences — formatted for the next project decision, not just for the file.

Who We Work With

For Landowners

For Developers
