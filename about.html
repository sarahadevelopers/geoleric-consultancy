Who We Are

A Consultancy Built Around Precision, Compliance and Local Context.

GeoLeric Consultants is a professional physical planning, GIS mapping and environmental consultancy operating under executive leadership in Machakos County, Kenya. We specialise in urban development, land use and applied environmental science — translating spatial data into decisions that hold up before county authorities and national regulators alike.

Our work sits at the intersection of three disciplines that are usually handled separately: physical planning and zoning, geographic information systems, and environmental management. By holding all three in-house, we remove the friction that developers, landowners and municipal clients normally absorb when coordinating between multiple consultants.

From structural layout design and cadastral boundary verification to Environmental Impact Assessments and annual waste audits, every deliverable is produced to NEMA and county submission standards — the first time.
