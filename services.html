Cadastral Surveying

We undertake cadastral surveys for the purpose of land registration and property ownership documentation in accordance with the laws of Kenya — covering boundary surveys, subdivision surveys, boundary re-establishment and land registration across all applicable regulatory frameworks.

Boundary Surveys

Locate and mark property corners and boundary lines. Verify existing boundary evidence such as monuments, deeds and previous surveys.

Subdivision Surveys

Divide a large tract of land into smaller parcels. Prepare plans for residential, commercial or agricultural developments.

Boundary Re-establishment

Restore lost or damaged boundary markers. Reconstruct boundaries using legal and historical records.

Land Registration

Provide accurate survey data for land title registration. Assist government land administration systems.

Land Registration Frameworks

We undertake cadastral surveys for the purpose of land registration and property ownership documentation in accordance with the laws of Kenya. Our services cover surveys required under all applicable land registration frameworks, including:

  • The former Registration of Titles Act (RTA)
  • Registered Land Act (RLA)
  • National Land Commission Act
  • Government Land Act (GLA)
  • The current Land Registration Act
  • Sectional Properties Act (SPA)
  • Physical and Land Use Planning Act
  • Land Act, and Survey Act
Importance of Cadastral Surveying
  • Protects property rights
  • Prevents and resolves land disputes
  • Supports taxation and land valuation
  • Facilitates land development and urban planning
  • Provides a legal basis for land ownership
Deliverables
  • Boundary survey plans
  • Subdivision scheme drawings
  • Boundary re-establishment reports
  • Land registration survey data
  • Deed plans & beacon records

How We Work

The Engagement Pathway

A structured five-stage workflow that takes your project from initial enquiry through to a fully documented, compliance-ready close.

01

Initial Brief

We take your site details, objectives and any existing documentation, and clarify the exact scope required.

02

Site Assessment

Field visit, boundary verification and spatial data capture using RTK GNSS, total station or UAV, as applicable.

03

Technical Analysis

Processing in GIS and CAD — terrain, parcels, constraints and planning context analysed into a defensible proposal.

04

Documentation

Drawing packs, planning justification reports or EIA studies compiled to submission standard.

05

Submission & Follow-Through

Lodgement with county or NEMA, response to queries, and support through to final approval.

Timelines depend on project scale and regulatory queues, but we scope and communicate them clearly at the brief stage — no ambiguity.
