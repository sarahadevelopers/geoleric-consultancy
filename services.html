Utilising Geographic Information Systems to process spatial data, map terrain boundaries and analyse structural growth patterns — turning raw coordinates into decisions your project can act on.

Digital mapping of land parcel boundaries, registration blocks and cadastral frameworks — reconciled against registry records for accurate, defensible parcel geometry.

Topographic Analysis

High-resolution aerial capture producing orthomosaics, digital surface models and site progress records — ideal for large parcels and ongoing construction verification.

Geographic Database Management

Spatial Growth Analysis

Comparative analysis of structural growth and land-use change across time — used by developers and county agencies to anticipate infrastructure demand.

Environmental Consulting

Effluent Discharge Plans

Preparation of effluent discharge licence applications and supporting technical documentation for industrial and commercial facilities discharging into the environment.

Environmental Management Plans (EMP)

Common Questions

What Clients Ask Us First

If your question is not covered here, reach out directly and we will answer it against your specific site.

Ask a Direct Question

How long does a subdivision scheme usually take?

It depends on parcel size, access requirements and current county approval queues. We scope the technical work at the brief stage and give you a realistic window rather than an optimistic one.

Do I need an EIA before I can start construction?

Most projects listed under the Second Schedule of EMCA require either a full EIA licence or a Project Report. If you share your project type and location, we can advise within the first conversation which applies.

Can you work on a site outside Machakos County?

Yes. Our core operational base is Machakos, but we regularly extend mapping and environmental work to projects elsewhere in Kenya. Field logistics are quoted separately where travel is significant.

What do you need from me to start?

At minimum: the plot or L.R. number, location (sub-county), and a short description of what you want to achieve. If you have a title deed, deed plan or previous correspondence, include it — it speeds things up.

Do you handle submissions to NEMA and the county on my behalf?

Yes. Submission and follow-through are part of the engagement, not an optional extra. If a reviewer raises a query, we respond to it — you are not left chasing the file yourself.
